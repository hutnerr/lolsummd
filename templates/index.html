Help

Everything you need to get started

Overview

lolsummd lets you combine the champion mastery data of multiple League of Legends accounts into one unified summary.

You can add up to {{ max_accounts }} accounts at a time from any region.

✦

Instructions

Add accounts by entering a username and tag. For example, SummonerName#NA1 splits into SummonerName and NA1.

Alternatively, you can copy paste in something like username#tag and it will split them for you.

The TAG field will default to the standard tag for the selected region if left blank.

Once you've added your accounts, click Get Combined Mastery to retrieve the aggregated data.

Remove individual accounts with the Remove button, or wipe the whole list with Clear All.

✦

Sharing

Click Share Link to copy a URL that pre-loads your current account list for anyone who opens it.

Your accounts are also saved automatically in your browser. When you return to the site, your last used accounts will be restored so you don't need to re-enter them.
